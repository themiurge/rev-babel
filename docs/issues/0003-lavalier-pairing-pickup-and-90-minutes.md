# 0003. Lavalier: pairing, pickup, and 90 minutes

- **Status:** open
- **Owner:** 🧑 Emilio
- **Milestone:** M2
- **Opened:** 2026-09-05

## Context

The microphone is also the speaker gate (see issue 0011): only the
teacher is transcribed because only the teacher is miked. How well that
holds is a property of the hardware, not the code. Battery life and
pairing stability over a full lesson are unknowns.

## What it takes

Wear it, speak, and have someone talk from the back of the room while
recording. Check what the recording picks up. Confirm the pairing survives
90 minutes and the phone can stay on power.

## Done when

A 90-minute recording exists, with a note on how audible a voice from across the room is.
